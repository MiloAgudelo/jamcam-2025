Get ready for adventure at JamCam 2025! This inter-American event will bring together thousands of young Scouts from across the region in Cali, Colombia, from December 27, 2025 to January 2, 2026. It will be a unique opportunity to experience Scout brotherhood, learn and immerse yourself in vibrant Colombian culture.

JamCam will be held at Centro Campestre Comfandi Pance, an ideal space surrounded by the natural beauty of Cali, the world's salsa capital. This gathering aims to strengthen bonds, foster learning and adventure in a multicultural environment, offering unforgettable experiences for each participant.

Under the motto "Americas United for Peace", JamCam 2025 aligns with the UN's call to promote peace and trust. The event will focus on values such as cooperation, empathy, solidarity and freedom, seeking to inspire new generations to be agents of positive change.

JamCam 2025 is more than a camp; it's a transformative platform where diversity, adventure and peacebuilding converge. Participants, young people aged 11 to 17 and adult volunteers from the International Service Team (IST), will enjoy a rich and varied program. From adventure activities, sports and community service, to workshops on Sustainable Development Goals, cultural exploration in Cali and vibrant night events, each day promises to be a unique experience. Inspired by Colombia's biodiversity and its birds, the program will promote inclusion, sustainability and youth leadership, weaving lasting friendships and a renewed commitment to Scout Movement values to build a more just and fraternal future together.

Activities and Program

Adventure and Exploration

Experience adventure to the fullest with action-packed activities. From adventure circuits and sports to local exploration, you'll discover Cali's charms while testing your skills in a safe and exciting environment.

Development and Service

Participate in activities focused on inclusion, diversity, and sustainable development. In the Inter-American Development Village, you'll contribute to the SDGs and leave a positive footprint through community service and cultural exchange.

Night Life

When the sun sets, the magic continues with ceremonies, shows, campfires, and an unforgettable New Year's celebration. Plus, you'll have access to permanent activity points to complement your experience.

Americas United for Peace: An Event with Purpose

Explore and Discover

Immerse yourself in the JamCam 2025 adventure. This is the time to open your eyes to new experiences, learn from other cultures, and connect with nature. Explore your surroundings, make new friendships, and prepare for days full of excitement and learning.

Share and Build

Every story, every idea, and every action can make a difference. Share what you know, learn from others, and work as a team to create unforgettable moments. Together, we'll build an environment of respect, solidarity, and growth.

Transform and Leave a Mark

Change begins with small actions. Be inspired by the experiences lived and take them beyond the camp. Apply what you've learned in your community and become an agent of peace. Your impact can reach further than you imagine.
